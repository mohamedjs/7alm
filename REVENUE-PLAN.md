### Kill ThemeForest. Here's why.

ThemeForest rewards **visual design polish** competing against Ukrainian and Indonesian studios who do nothing else, at $39/sale, with a 3–8 week review queue and a 50–70% cut to Envato. A new author's realistic first-year item does $500–2,000/**month** — and that's a *good* outcome.

Your moat is **Arabic RTL + automation + AI**. ThemeForest scores none of that. You would be competing on your weakest axis for the smallest prize. Every hour there returns 10–20x more in channel #1.

*Revisit in month 6 — as a lead magnet, not a business.*

### YouTube: right instinct, wrong scoreboard

Arabic AI/automation content has **almost no serious competition** and the Gulf audience pays well. But:
- AdSense on Arabic tech: ~$1–3 RPM. 100K views ≈ $150. Meaningless.
- Monetization threshold + algorithm ramp: 4–8 months.

**So don't measure YouTube in AdSense. Measure it in booked calls.** One video — *"شوف إزاي بعمل حملة إعلانية كاملة من على التليجرام"* — showing your Telegram bot researching a niche, writing the campaign, generating the image and publishing to Instagram, is the best sales asset you will ever make. It closes deals while you sleep. That's the ROI, and it starts in week 3, not month 8.

## 5. What will actually go wrong

Not theoretical — these are the ones that hit:

1. **You'll retreat into code.** A feature feels productive; a cold DM feels awful. Building is the comfortable failure mode and it will quietly eat all eight weeks. **The platform is done. Ship configurations, not features.**
2. **You'll underprice out of fear.** The first "ده غالي" will tempt you to drop to 10K. Don't. Cheap clients demand the most and refer the worst. Hold the price; let them walk.
3. **Support will swallow you.** Twenty clients WhatsApping at midnight ends the sales motion. Fixed support hours in the contract from client #1, not client #10.
4. **Scope creep.** "ممكن كمان تعمل...". Everything outside the tier is a paid change request. Write it into the contract.
5. **You'll chase all six channels at once.** This is the real killer. **Channels #1 and #2 are 74% of the target.** Do those. #3 and #5 only in leftover hours. #6 not at all.
